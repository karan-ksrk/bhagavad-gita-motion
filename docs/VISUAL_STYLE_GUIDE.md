# Visual Style Guide

Applies to all generated artwork, video, and in-app UI. Characters and
locations: `CHARACTER_BIBLE.md`.

## Look

Cinematic Indian mythological painting reimagined as a film still:
painterly realism, soft volumetric light, fine ornament detail. Calm,
reverent, dignified. Restrained composition.

## Palette

Matches the app's colour tokens (`lib/core/theme/app_colors.dart`).

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|------|--------|-----|
| Deep background / shadow | Midnight blue | `#0B1426` |
| Neutral dark | Charcoal | `#1B1B21` |
| Primary accent / highlight | Antique gold | `#C9A227` |
| Rim light / secondary | Celestial blue | `#7EB0E6` |
| Light tones / text | Warm ivory | `#F3E9D2` |

Battlefield scenes add warm dust ochres and sky blues; keep gold and
midnight blue dominant.

## Lighting

- Key light: warm gold, usually upper left on Krishna and upper right on
  Arjuna (as in the masters).
- Rim light: soft celestial blue on the shadow side.
- Golden hour for battlefield exteriors; deep midnight blue for
  portraits and divine moments.
- No harsh flashes or strobing; motion work must stay gentle.

## Composition (production scene art)

- **Vertical 9:16** for all app scenes (generate at the tallest
  portrait size available, then extend or crop to 9:16).
- **Bottom 35% clear** of faces and key detail: this is where dialogue
  and shloka text sit.
- Main subject in the upper two-thirds.
- Leave space around Krishna's head and shoulders for aura and particle
  effects; record effect anchor coordinates per scene.
- Layered-motion scenes: character on a transparent background,
  environment as a separate plate.

## Hard rules

- No text, letters, numbers, pseudo-script, or symbols that read as
  writing anywhere in any image or video. Zoom in on banners, borders,
  jewellery, and armour before approval.
- No cartoon, anime, chibi, or game styling.
- No gore or graphic violence.
- Correct hands (five fingers, natural grip).
- Every production image references the relevant approved master.
