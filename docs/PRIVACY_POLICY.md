# Moksharan Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-04

Moksharan ("the app") presents the Bhagavad Gita as an illustrated,
animated reader. This policy explains what the app does with
information. In short: **the app does not collect, transmit, or share
any personal information.**

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your device, in the app's private
storage:

- Your settings: language, whether transliteration is shown, and
  reduced motion.
- Your reading progress: the last scene read and which scenes you have
  viewed.
- Your bookmarks.
- Chapter media you choose to download.

This information never leaves your device. The developer cannot see it.
You can clear reading progress in Settings, remove bookmarks in
Bookmarks, and remove downloaded chapters in Settings, then Storage.
Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes
all of it.

## Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics, crash
reporting, or tracking. It does not access your contacts, location,
camera, microphone, photos, or files.

## Chapter downloads

Some chapters' pictures and videos are downloaded when you ask for
them. The Google Play Store app delivers these downloads (Play Asset
Delivery). Moksharan does not run its own servers. Google's handling of
Play Store downloads is described in Google's privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including
children, because it collects no information at all.

## Changes

If the app's data practices change, for example if optional crash
reporting is added, this policy will be updated before that version is
released, and the effective date above will change.
