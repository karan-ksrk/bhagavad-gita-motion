## Full description (test release)

```text
Moksharan presents the Bhagavad Gita as a living visual experience: full-screen cinematic scenes, gentle layered motion, and manga panels you tap through at your own pace.

EARLY TEST VERSION
This build contains sample scenes from Chapters 1 and 2. Verse text and translations are placeholders until they are checked against their sources. Every unverified line is clearly marked in the app.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Read scene by scene. Swipe between cinematic scenes, layered-motion scenes, and manga panels.
• Open any verse. Sanskrit is always shown, with transliteration you can turn on or off.
• Choose Hindi or English for the app and for translations.
• Bookmark verses and scenes. Pick up where you left off.
• Download chapters when you need them; remove them to free space. Your bookmarks and progress stay.
• Reduce motion if you prefer still images.

RESPECTFUL AND CLEAR
Scripture and creative retelling are labelled separately, so you always know which is which. Verse numbering follows the Gita Press Gorakhpur edition; the English translation is K. T. Telang (1882).

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything you save stays on your device, and downloaded chapters work offline.

Artwork and motion were created with AI image and video tools and directed by the developer.
```
