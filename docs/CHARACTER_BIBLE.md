## Krishna

**Master:** `krishna_master_v001.png`

| Feature | Canonical |
|---------|-----------|
| Age, build | Youthful man in his twenties; slender, graceful |
| Complexion | Soft blue with a cool lavender undertone, gentle sheen (D-122) |
| Eyes | Warm brown, almond-shaped, calm |
| Expression range | Calm, gently speaking, compassionate; faint smile at rest |
| Hair | Long, dark brown, loose waves past the shoulders |
| Crown | Tall gold mukut with filigree, pearls, and a central red teardrop gem |
| Peacock feather | One feather, upright, tucked at the crown's front-left (viewer's left in front view) |
| Earrings | Gold drop earrings with a pearl |
| Necklace | Gold collar necklace with a central red teardrop gem |
| Garland | Long garland of white and pink flowers, reaching the knees in full body |
| Upper garment | Gold silk scarf (angavastram) over the left shoulder |
| Lower garment | Yellow silk dhoti (pitambara) with gold border; jewelled gold belt |
| Arms | Gold armlets with red gem; gold bracelets |
| Feet | Bare |
| On the chariot | Seated at the front as charioteer, reins in both hands |

Views: `krishna_threequarter_v001.png`, `krishna_profile_v001.png`

`krishna_chariot_seat_v001.png`, `krishna_expressions_v001.png`,
`krishna_crown_detail_v001.png`.

dark skin tones other than the approved blue, aggressive expression.

## Chariot

**Master:** `chariot_master_v001.png`; positions: `chariot_seats_v001.png`

- Gold-clad wooden body with floral filigree; two large spoked wheels
  with gold rims and hubs.
- Domed gold canopy over the rear.
- Red velvet seats: warrior seat at the rear, charioteer seat at the
  front.
- Four white horses in gold harness with red gems and tassels.
- Saffron banner with a pale monkey emblem on a tall gold pole at the
  rear.

Known issue (D-123): both chariot references show a small glyph-like
mark on the banner beside the monkey. The references are kept as-is.
In production art, generate the banner without that mark and check it
at zoom before approval.
